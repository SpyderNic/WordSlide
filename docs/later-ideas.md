# Word Slide — Later Ideas

Ideas the designer wants to remember for after the prototype. None of these are planned or approved for building yet.

- **Forgive accent mix-ups.** Be kind to players by swapping an accented letter for its plain letter, or the other way round, such as E for É, É for E, or Á for A. From [story 6, R3](example-maps/story-06-check-and-lock-a-word.md). The prototype keeps them as different letters (D21).
- **Generate levels when the content runs out.** Once a player reaches the end of the authored levels, new ones are generated. From [story 11, R11](example-maps/story-11-next-level-and-progress.md). This goes beyond the offline generator in story 14: it would run on the player's device, so the generator, its checks and its word lists would ship with the game, and nobody could approve each level.
- **Bonus words.** Valid words the puzzle doesn't expect, found horizontally and vertically within the letter set and cell constraints. This was story 13. It needs a dictionary shipped with the game, with a word list and a profanity filter, and it has to fit with "only intended answers lock" (D8).
- **Tutorial for bonus words.** A short tutorial kicks in when the player finds their first bonus word. From [story 12, R1](example-maps/story-12-first-level-onboarding.md).
- **Difficulty tiers.** Easy, medium and hard levels have their own limits on rows and word length, and extra letters in the pool. From [story 12, Q1](example-maps/story-12-first-level-onboarding.md). Extra letters are the red herrings from the design document.
