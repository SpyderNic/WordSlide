# Word Slide — Later Ideas

Ideas the designer wants to remember for after the prototype. None of these are planned or approved for building yet.

- **Forgive accent mix-ups.** Be kind to players by swapping an accented letter for its plain letter, or the other way round, such as E for É, É for E, or Á for A. From [story 6, R3](example-maps/story-06-check-and-lock-a-word.md). The prototype keeps them as different letters (D21).
- **Generate levels when the content runs out.** Once a player reaches the end of the authored levels, new ones are generated. From [story 11, R11](example-maps/story-11-next-level-and-progress.md). This goes beyond the offline generator in story 14: it would run on the player's device, so the generator, its checks and its word lists would ship with the game, and nobody could approve each level.
- **Bonus words beyond the theme.** *Story 25 covers themed bonus words in the column.* Valid words the puzzle doesn't expect, found horizontally and vertically within the letter set and cell constraints. This was story 13. It needs a dictionary shipped with the game, with a word list and a profanity filter, and it has to fit with "only intended answers lock" (D8).
- **A collection of bonus words.** Every bonus word found goes into an album per theme, such as "Animals: 37 found". From the bonus words discussion on 2026-11-04. Bonus words themselves are now story 25, for the column only and from the theme's word list. To avoid any fear of missing out, an album would never show what's left to find in a level, since won levels can't be replayed.
- **Tutorial for bonus words.** A short tutorial kicks in when the player finds their first bonus word. From [story 12, R1](example-maps/story-12-first-level-onboarding.md).
- **Difficulty tiers.** Easy, medium and hard levels have their own limits on rows and word length, and extra letters in the pool. From [story 12, Q1](example-maps/story-12-first-level-onboarding.md). Extra letters are the red herrings from the design document.
- **Hints for watching ads.** On CrazyGames, hints could be earned by watching ads, which might replace revealed letters in levels. From [story 14, Q3](example-maps/story-14-level-generator.md). Now in the backlog as stories 19 (hints) and 20 (hint currency and ads).
- **Introduce reordering later.** Vertical reordering could arrive on later levels, not level 1, so levels aren't too difficult. From [story 14, Q4](example-maps/story-14-level-generator.md). The game is ready for this (D45), but it would change the tutorial (story 12, steps 3 and 4).
- **Other languages.** English is for the prototype, and the game may need to cover more languages eventually. From [story 15, R4](example-maps/story-15-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Let the generator take over.** The designer curates levels until they're happy for the generator to take over, and might review an occasional level after that. From [story 15, R7](example-maps/story-15-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Recurring themes.** The same theme can come back later, for example Animals as theme 1 and theme 8, perhaps called Animals II. From [story 15, Q7](example-maps/story-15-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Sub category, as a further hint**. For example, "Animals" as a theme, and "Birds" as a sub category, or "Split Words" as a theme, and "Sun" as a sub category (For words like Glasses, Cream, Bathing... since it'd be split words of Sunglasses, Sun cream and Sunbathing)
- **Level selector grouped by theme.** A main menu or level selector could group levels by theme. From [story 15, Q7](example-maps/story-15-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Full art style.** Once the game proves fun, the look could use a licensed asset pack, or an artist or AI image tool. From [story 16, Q4](example-maps/story-16-visual-style.md).
- **Choosing styles.** Players could change the game's style, and maybe unlock styles with a currency. From [story 16, R2](example-maps/story-16-visual-style.md). The prototype has one style, but it's built so that this is easy to add.
