# Word Slide — Later Ideas

Ideas the designer wants to remember for after the prototype. None of these are planned or approved for building yet.

- **Forgive accent mix-ups.** Be kind to players by swapping an accented letter for its plain letter, or the other way round, such as E for É, É for E, or Á for A. From [M03-04, R3](milestones/3-phase-one/m03-04-check-and-lock-a-word.md). The prototype keeps them as different letters (D21).
- **Generate levels when the content runs out.** Once a player reaches the end of the authored levels, new ones are generated. From [M05-01, R11](milestones/5-the-whole-game/m05-01-next-level-and-progress.md). This goes beyond the offline generator in M06-01: it would run on the player's device, so the generator, its checks and its word lists would ship with the game, and nobody could approve each level.
- **Bonus words beyond the theme.** *M07-05 covers themed bonus words in the column.* Valid words the puzzle doesn't expect, found horizontally and vertically within the letter set and cell constraints. This was story 13. It needs a dictionary shipped with the game, with a word list and a profanity filter, and it has to fit with "only intended answers lock" (D8).
- **A collection of bonus words.** Every bonus word found goes into an album per theme, such as "Animals: 37 found". From the bonus words discussion on 2026-11-04. Bonus words themselves are now M07-05, for the column only and from the theme's word list. To avoid any fear of missing out, an album would never show what's left to find in a level, since won levels can't be replayed.
- **Tutorial for bonus words.** A short tutorial kicks in when the player finds their first bonus word. From [M05-03, R1](milestones/5-the-whole-game/m05-03-first-level-onboarding.md).
- **Difficulty tiers.** Easy, medium and hard levels have their own limits on rows and word length, and extra letters in the pool. From [M05-03, Q1](milestones/5-the-whole-game/m05-03-first-level-onboarding.md). Extra letters are the red herrings from the design document.
- **Declutter hint.** In levels with red herring letters, a hint that removes them from the selected word's pool. From the designer's list of hints, on 2026-11-05 (M07-01).
- **Hints for watching ads.** On CrazyGames, hints could be earned by watching ads, which might replace revealed letters in levels. From [M06-01, Q3](milestones/6-content/m06-01-level-generator.md). Now in the backlog as M07-01 (hints) and 20 (hint currency and ads).
- **Introduce reordering later.** Vertical reordering could arrive on later levels, not level 1, so levels aren't too difficult. From [M06-01, Q4](milestones/6-content/m06-01-level-generator.md). The game is ready for this (D45), but it would change the tutorial (M05-03, steps 3 and 4).
- **Other languages.** English is for the prototype, and the game may need to cover more languages eventually. From [M06-02, R4](milestones/6-content/m06-02-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Let the generator take over.** The designer curates levels until they're happy for the generator to take over, and might review an occasional level after that. From [M06-02, R7](milestones/6-content/m06-02-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Recurring themes.** The same theme can come back later, for example Animals as theme 1 and theme 8, perhaps called Animals II. From [M06-02, Q7](milestones/6-content/m06-02-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Sub category, as a further hint**. For example, "Animals" as a theme, and "Birds" as a sub category, or "Split Words" as a theme, and "Sun" as a sub category (For words like Glasses, Cream, Bathing... since it'd be split words of Sunglasses, Sun cream and Sunbathing)
- **Level selector grouped by theme.** A main menu or level selector could group levels by theme. From [M06-02, Q7](milestones/6-content/m06-02-draft-the-first-levels.md).
- **Full art style.** Once the game proves fun, the look could use a licensed asset pack, or an artist or AI image tool. From [M05-02, Q4](milestones/5-the-whole-game/m05-02-visual-style.md).
- **Daily rewards or daily tasks**, much later: from the economy discussion on 2026-11-05 (D51).
- **A shop, if there are in-app purchases.** The coin counter becomes the shop: a "+" next to it, and the whole panel opens the shop when tapped. The ad button is built to be reused there. From [M07-02, Q1 and Q12](milestones/7-worth-playing/m07-02-currency-and-ads.md).
- **Choosing styles.** Players could change the game's style, and maybe unlock styles with a currency. From [M05-02, R2](milestones/5-the-whole-game/m05-02-visual-style.md). The prototype has one style, but it's built so that this is easy to add.
- **Phase 2 typing.** Typing a letter in phase 2 should make the selected word's typed letter travel to the cryptex location, if it exists within the word. Nothing happens if it's not in.
