## Out of scope for the first playable version

From the design-context document; not to be built unless the designer asks:
red herrings, power-ups, chapters and their content, daily challenges, async challenges/multiplayer, CrazyGames SDK integration, bonus words (story 13), moved out on 2026-10-31 (themed bonus words are now story 25); see [later ideas](later-ideas.md).

There are no stars: they were replaced by 1 point per level completed (D23).
